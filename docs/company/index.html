Company

Built for decisions that matter

Aube Research & Analytics is an independent patent research practice. We research, chart and analyze patents, with a focus on Wi-Fi and 3GPP standards, so law firms, funders and IP teams can act with confidence.

Our story

Built to bring more rigor to patent research

Aube was founded on a simple premise: patent research that supports a real legal or business decision needs more rigor than a database search. AI-assisted internal tooling helps with triage and first-pass mapping, but the judgment on every case comes from a person who understands the technology.

Our focus is standards-essential work on Wi-Fi (IEEE 802.11) and 3GPP, backed by hardware testing and product teardowns where the evidence has to come from the device itself. We also work on Qi wireless charging and media standards such as HEVC, and take on semiconductors, materials, biotech and university pre-filing work where the scope fits.

Standards

What we read every day

Wi-Fi (IEEE 802.11)

802.11ax/be and earlier amendments: MAC and PHY procedures, multi-link operation, OFDMA scheduling.

3GPP cellular

LTE and 5G NR specifications across RAN and core, charted by release and version.

Qi wireless charging

Wireless Power Consortium specifications: power transfer, communication and foreign-object detection.

HEVC / media standards

Video coding standards such as HEVC (H.265): coding tools, syntax and decoding processes.
