Process

How every engagement runs

  1. Scoping

    A 30-minute call to pin down the decision, the patents, the standard sections and the deliverable, before any work starts.

  2. Search & charting

    Patent and non-patent literature search, then element-by-element charting against the specification or product evidence.

  3. Technical review

    Every mapping is checked against the source text. Evidence basis and confidence are stated per element, not implied.

  4. Written report

    Chart, findings and reasoning in a report your counsel can use, signed off with the reviewer’s name and date.
